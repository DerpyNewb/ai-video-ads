# AI video ads

Eight spec video ads for fictional brands, made with Google Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni and finished in DaVinci Resolve, at 16:9, 9:16 for Reels and Stories, and 1:1 for the feed. Four show the product in live action; the other four are stop-motion clay, a creator-style phone video, a short story with dialogue, and a 1990s TV spot. Every clip is AI-generated, including the people; none of this is client work.

The page is `index.html`; the videos in `media/` are web copies of the delivered files.
