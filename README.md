# AI video ads

Eight spec video ads for brands that don't exist, at 16:9, 9:16 for Reels and Stories, and 1:1 for the feed. Google Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni made the clips, Flow Music made the music, and DaVinci Resolve put each one together. Four show off the product in live action; the other four are stop-motion clay, a creator-style phone video, a little story with dialogue, and a 1990s TV spot. Every clip is AI-generated, people included, and so is the music. None of it is client work.

The page is `index.html`, and the videos in `media/` are web copies of the delivered files.
